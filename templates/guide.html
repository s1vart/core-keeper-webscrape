Core Keeper Combat Guide

DPS Calculation

Understanding how damage is calculated in Core Keeper is crucial for optimizing your builds.

Basic DPS Formula

DPS = (Base Damage × Damage Multiplier) × Attack Speed × (1 + Crit Chance × Crit Multiplier)
Pro Tip

When choosing between weapons with similar DPS, consider the attack speed. Faster weapons give you more mobility and flexibility in combat.

Combat Stats Explained

Base Damage The raw damage output of your weapon before any multipliers
Attack Speed How many attacks you can perform per second
Crit Chance Probability of landing a critical hit
Crit Multiplier Damage multiplier applied on critical hits

Stat Caps

Stat Maximum Value Notes
Attack Speed 2.5/s Can be exceeded with buffs
Crit Chance 100% Hard cap
Damage Multiplier None Stacks multiplicatively

Build Tips

Early Game

Starting Out

Focus on weapons with consistent damage output rather than high crit chance early on. Upgrade your weapon whenever possible.

Mid Game

Optimization

Start incorporating food and potion buffs into your build. Look for gear with complementary stats.

Late Game

Min-Maxing

Focus on maximizing your primary damage source while maintaining a good balance of utility stats.
